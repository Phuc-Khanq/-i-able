# big3lr epoch 1: three times the data at the new settings ties big1, but doesn't beat 8,000

big3lr is big3s16lr with **one change: big3's 24,000 examples instead of big3s's 8,000** (`data/cleanup_train/big3_train.jsonl`,
5,752 different answers; big3s is a random 8,000 of it). Everything else is the same: rank 16 / alpha 32, learning rate
1e-4, 2 epochs planned, warm-up and cosine decay, seed 1234, whole sentences held back (22,793 trained, 1,207 held back),
the same 400 watched test cases. It started 2026-11-05 at 18:13 and saved its epoch-1 adapter at 21:56 (step 2,849,
about 223 min).

**At 22:27 the PC restarted** (the Kaspersky installer asked Windows to restart; System log event 1074), which killed the run
at step 3,163 of 5,698. The trainer can't resume, so only the epoch-1 adapter exists. This review scores it. Like
big3s16lr's epoch 1, it is the halfway point of a two-epoch cosine schedule, so the two compare like for like.

Scoring as the app runs it: llama.cpp b11960, Q8_0, greedy, prompt `cleanup_t1_short`, Khang's PC (RTX 5080), the unchanged
big1_test.csv (sha256 ec2b1b0aa648c0e3). A game started at 23:25, during the test run, so its latency is not comparable;
the answers are (greedy decoding gives the same answer whatever else runs).

Head to head on the fair 2,301 (automatic, not hand-checked with Khang's rule):

- **Against big3s16lr epoch 1 (only the data differs): wins 3, loses 9.** The same answer word for word on 2,291 of 2,500.
- Against big1: wins 7, loses 7.
- Against big3 (the same 24,000 at the old settings): wins 28, loses 3.

**How sure is this?** 9 to 3 against big3s16lr could come from chance about 15% of the time, so "not better" is safe and
"worse" is not proven. 28 to 3 against big3 is far beyond chance.

Of big3's extra 16,000 examples, only 6 of the 199 "gap" cases had all their terms covered, and none of the 94 "no run
trained on" cases. So the extra data is mostly more of the same sentence banks, as planned for this test.

For comparison, test-case loss: big3s16lr epoch 1 0.0192 (its lowest 0.0187), big1 final 0.0190, big3 (old settings) 0.0326
at step 500 and 0.0279 at the end.

- **The new settings fixed most of what went wrong with big3.** The same 24,000 examples now reach 0.0173 to 0.0235 on test
  cases instead of 0.028 to 0.033. "Three times the data made it worse" was mostly the rank and learning rate.
- **The test-case loss swings between checks** (0.0173 at step 1,500, 0.0235 at 2,000, 0.0176 at 2,500, 0.0213 at the save).
  The held-back loss moves with it (0.0094, 0.0130, 0.0083, 0.0109), so the model itself moves, not only the measure: at
  learning rate 1e-4 with batches of 8, the weights still wander mid-schedule. The saved adapter landed on a high point. A
  run that ends its cosine decay at epoch 1 (learning rate down to 0) would settle; that is the one-epoch test.
- The two lowest test-case losses of any run were measured here (steps 1,500 and 2,500), but no adapter was saved there, and
  picking a checkpoint by these 400 test cases would leak the test into the choice. So they are a hint, not a result.

## What changed in the answers

**The medical words:**

| Heard | big1 | big3s16lr epoch 1 | big3lr epoch 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the dog's hard worm pill | heartworm | hard worm (as heard) | **heartworm** |
| the hard worm test came back negative | hard worm (as heard) | hardware (wrong) | **heartworm** |
| I finished the pred knee zone | prednisone | physical therapy (wrong) | **prednisone** ("prednisone course") |
| the afo on my rite I mean foot | afo | foam (wrong) | foam (wrong) |
| the half oh needs a new strap | backpack (wrong) | harness (wrong) | harness (wrong) |
| an accessible you uh knit | unit | unit | **elevator** (wrong) |

**Lost against big3s16lr epoch 1 (9):** four clean sentences get "the" or "please" added ("I can't hear you, please check
your mic", "the home care hours", "a thickener", "the estimated taxes"); "autopay" missed twice ("automatic payment", and
"Please note that I ought to pay for the electric bill"); "wheelchair fan" became "wheelchair lift" (meant: van);
"accessible unit" became "elevator"; "double you two" (W-2) became "1099".

**Won (3):** "My W-2 from the old job hasn't arrived yet" (big3s16lr kept "W2 from my old job"), one clean sentence big3s16lr
changed ("I have receipts for the wheelchair ramp"), and one ambiguous case.

The "1099" answer is flagged as an invented fact by the scorer (numbers nobody said), and the app's guard (`check_cleanup`)
blocks a number nobody said, so in the app the user would see their own words instead. "Elevator" and "lift" are not
caught: they are confident wrong guesses with no new fact.

## What it means

1. **More of the same data no longer hurts much, but it doesn't help.** At the new settings, three times the examples ties
   big1 and stays a little behind the 8,000-example run. It needs three times the training time (223 min to epoch 1 vs
   about 75). For the 100,000 goal: copies and noise passes over the same sentence banks are not worth it; new sentences are.
2. **The "add a the" habit comes back with more copies** (clean sentences changed 7 vs 4). More passes over the
   `drop_small_words` examples teach it harder.
3. **big3s16lr epoch 1 stays the proposed candidate.** No change to the app.
4. **Not settled:** whether 24,000 does better when its schedule ends at epoch 1. The loss checks hint it might (two
   checks below every earlier run), but the swing between checks is as large as the difference.
