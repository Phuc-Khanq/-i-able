# big2 against big1

big2 is the overnight run of 2026-11-03/04: a LoRA adapter on Qwen3.5-4B with twice big1's capacity (rank 32 /
alpha 64; big1 16 / 32; both train every linear layer), 11,400 training examples plus 600 held back for the loss
(`data/cleanup_train/big2_train.jsonl`, `bigset.py --more-from data/cleanup_train/big1_test.csv`), 3 epochs, 4,275
steps, 344.1 min on the 5080 (peak 10.19 GB). Held-back loss 0.0861 before, 0.0118 after epoch 1, 0.0047 after
epoch 2, 0.0040 at the end. All runs as the app runs them: llama.cpp b11960, Q8_0, greedy, prompt
`cleanup_t1_short`, Khang's PC (RTX 5080). The test file is the unchanged big1_test.csv (sha256 ec2b1b0aa648c0e3).

## Result: big2 does not beat big1. Keep big1.

| | big1 | big2 epoch 1 | big2 epoch 2 | big2 final |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| big1 test, 2,500, automatic | **2,441** | 2,362 | 2,409 | 2,411 |
| Clean sentences changed (of 378) | **4** | 29 | 15 | 14 |
| Silent guesses (of 380) | **0** | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Possible invented facts flagged | 8 | 18 | 9 | 6 |
| Broken JSON | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Same 150 random cases, full pass by Khang's rule | **141** | | | 139 |
| Dev, 67, automatic | **65** | 64 | 62 | 62 |
| Latency on the 2,500, p50 / p90 / p99 (ms) | 289 / 338 / 444 | 289 / 330 / 443 | 289 / 327 / 442 | 290 / 332 / 441 |

No epoch of big2 is ahead of big1 on anything except latency, and that difference is noise. Twice the rank costs
nothing in speed or file size (the adapter is merged before export) but brought no gain.

## Why

**1. big2 never saw many of the test's terms; big1 did (explains about 9 of the 30 cases).** big2's data drops every
example that mentions a test-side term or its mishearing (685 of the pool). big1's data had no such filter, so terms
like W-2, referral, heartworm, thickener and curbside pickup appeared in its training, in other sentences. Of the 29
terms in the cases big1 passed and big2 failed, 26 never appear in big2's training. 193 test cases have a term that
big1 saw and big2 didn't: big1 160, big2 151. On the other 2,307 cases big1 still wins 2,281 to 2,260, and on the 94
cases whose terms neither saw it's 84 to 83. big2's number is the more honest one for new words; big1's 2,441 is a
little high.

**2. big2 adds small words to sentences that were fine (a real step back).** 14 of 378 clean sentences changed against
4: "please check your mic", "for the kids", "to visit", "the curbside pickup", "deploy" -> "deployment", and one
wrong word, "building manager" -> "billing manager". Epoch 1 was the worst (29). It's not the data mix: answers that
add "the" are 6.8% of big2's training against 6.3% of big1's, "please" 1.1% in both. Unmeasured whether it comes
from the higher rank, the longer training or the new field banks; only a run that changes one of them would tell.

**3. Broken questions got worse, not better, though big2 had 151 targeted examples for them.** In the 150: "Can we
roll back the last deploy?" -> "We rolled back the last deployment." "Can the lab results be shared..." -> "The lab
results will be shared...?" "Can I switch the sertraline to the evening?" -> "I switch to sertraline in the evening."
On dev: S20 "Dinner tonight is what we eat", X07 "When is class start tomorrow?", S22 "Pharmacy is close at nine".

## The 150 (seed 150, the same cases as the full1 and big1 hand checks)

132 of big2's answers are word for word big1's and keep big1's verdicts; the 18 that differ were judged by Claude with
Khang's rule. Fixed by big2 (3): B0376 hand controls (was "brake controls"), B1232 "the fifteenth of March" (was "the
end of March"), B1560 "The paper is back". Broken by big2 (5): B0362 and B1628 and B1414 above, B0618 "guest lift"
kept as heard, B1195 "Thank you to my followers" -> "Thank you". Still wrong in both: B0742 AFO (big2: "afro"),
B0058 Discord ("score server"), B1024 receipt, B1891 accessible unit, B1456, B1871, B2203. 141 - 5 + 3 = 139.

## Dev

Automatic 62 against 65. Newly failed: M02 "sex thirty" kept with "six thirty" only as an alternative, S11 "two
times" -> "twice" (meaning kept, the automatic check wants "two"), A10 one doctor's name picked without the other.
Of the other answers that differ from big1's, three read worse (S20, X07, S22 above) and one better (X01 "Please
cancel my doctor appointment for tomorrow."). Not reviewed blind; no Khang's-rule number for dev.

## The app's guard (check_cleanup, as of 212ca23) on the 2,500

big2: 16 blocked, 10 of them answers that passed the automatic checks; it catches 6 of big2's 89 automatic failures.
big1: 18 blocked, 9 good; catches 9 of 59. Most good answers blocked in both are dates from a misheard word ("by the
tend" -> "by the tenth", "dirtieth"), which the guard blocks on purpose (a number nobody said). New in big2: B1729 "your
graduation reunion" (a list word added, rightly blocked) and B2373 "Discord voice channel" (good, blocked as a name
added).
