# One epoch, and half the learning rate again: both tie big3s16lr; the settings have stopped mattering

Night chain 3 (2026-11-05/06) ran two tests of big3s16lr's recipe, each changing **one thing**:

- **big3s16lr1ep: one epoch instead of two.** The cosine schedule ends at the end of epoch 1, so the learning rate is down
  to 0 there. Question: is the end of the first pass the best point because of the schedule's second half, or just
  because it's one pass?
- **big3s16lr5: learning rate 5e-5 instead of 1e-4**, two epochs. Question: does an even gentler step do better still?

Everything else is big3s16lr's: big3s's 8,000 examples (7,598 trained, 402 whole sentences held back), rank 16 / alpha 32,
warm-up 10, seed 1234, the same 400 watched test cases. A game held the GPU until 00:03; then big3s16lr1ep trained from
00:03 to 01:28 (83.8 min), and big3s16lr5 from 01:48 to 04:37 (168.0 min; big3s16lr took 149.7 min, cause of the
difference not checked). Both were scored as the app runs them: llama.cpp b11960, Q8_0, greedy, prompt `cleanup_t1_short`,
Khang's PC (RTX 5080), the unchanged big1_test.csv (2,500 cases) and dev (67).

## The loss checks: lower loss, same score

| | Step | Training | Held-back sentences | Test cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| big3s16lr (1e-4, 2 epochs) | 949 (epoch 1) | 0.0021 | 0.0129 | 0.0192 |
| | 1,898 (end) | 0.0004 | 0.0135 | 0.0199 |
| big3s16lr1ep (1e-4, 1 epoch) | 500 | 0.0107 | 0.0150 | 0.0200 |
| | 949 (end) | 0.0034 | **0.0120** | 0.0170 |
| big3s16lr5 (5e-5, 2 epochs) | 500 | 0.0125 | 0.0165 | 0.0207 |
| | 949 (epoch 1) | 0.0026 | 0.0121 | **0.0169** |
| | 1,898 (end) | 0.0008 | 0.0128 | 0.0188 |

- **Ending the schedule at epoch 1, or a smaller step, gives the lowest losses on new data of any run** (held-back 0.0120,
  test cases 0.0169 to 0.0170; big3s16lr epoch 1: 0.0129 and 0.0192). The second epoch raises both again, at either
  learning rate, while the training loss keeps falling: the same memorizing as before.
- **But the lower loss doesn't turn into a higher score.** 0.0170 vs 0.0192 on the test cases, 2,445 vs 2,446 on the
  test. The loss is mostly about how sure the model is of answers it already gets right; the score turns on a few dozen
  hard cases, and those don't move.

## The hard cases flip between runs

The same handful of sound-alike cases decide the small differences, and each run gets a different few of them right:

| Heard | big1 | big3s16lr e1 | big3s16lr1ep | big3s16lr5 e1 | big3s16lr5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the dog's hard worm pill | heartworm | hard worm | heartworm | hard worm | heartworm |
| I finished the pred knee zone | prednisone | physical therapy | prednisone | prednisone | physical therapy |
| the wheelchair fan was late | fan | van | van | fan | van |
| my ought to pay failed | "obligation to pay" | autopay | "automatic payment" | autopay | autopay |
| an accessible you uh knit | unit | unit | elevator | unit | unit |
| the hard worm test | hard worm | hardware | hairworm | hard worm | Hawthorne |
| the afo on my rite I mean foot | afo | foam | foam | foam | foam |
| the half oh needs a new strap | backpack | harness | harness | harness | harness |

That is what noise looks like: these words are rare in the training data (heartworm, prednisone, AFO), so whether a run
gets them is close to a coin flip. A setting that really helped would get more of them right, not different ones.

## The safety details

- **big3s16lr1ep, dev 62 (3 below the others):** "Meeting not Tuesday it is Wednesday" became "The meeting is
  Wednesday." (the "not Tuesday" dropped); "Tell Alix I am coming" picked "Alex" without showing "Alice"; "take
  medicine two time day" became "twice a day". On the test it has one extra silent guess: "My grandson is seventy" became
  "seventeen".
- **big3s16lr5 final:** the same "not Tuesday" drop and "twice a day" on dev (63); "The hard worm test" became
  "The Hawthorne test" (a name nobody said); "forty no sorry two point one" became "2.1" (digits; the meaning is kept).
- **The app's guard (`check_cleanup`) blocks every one of these except "2.1"**, checked directly: "negation missing: not",
  "number added: seventeen", "name added: alex", "name added: hawthorne", "number missing: two". In the app the user
  would see their own words instead. "Twice a day" is a false alarm (same meaning).
- Dev is 67 cases and was tuned on, so 62 vs 65 is a warning sign, not proof. But big3s16lr epoch 1 has none of these.

## What it means

1. **The training settings have hit a plateau.** Five runs at rank 16 (learning rate 1e-4 or 5e-5, one or two epochs) all
   score 2,444 to 2,447, and none differs from another beyond chance. More tuning of the settings is unlikely to move the
   test. The limit is the data: rare words that appear in only a few training sentences.
2. **One epoch is enough for experiments:** the same score in half the time (84 vs 150 min). New data recipes can be
   tried with one-epoch runs.
3. **5e-5 is not better than 1e-4,** only slower to the same place. Keep 1e-4.
4. **The proposed candidate stays big3s16lr epoch 1.** It ties the best test score, and it has the best dev (65), no extra
   silent guess, no dropped negation and no digits. No change to the app.
5. **big3lr1ep (24,000 examples, one epoch, about 4 h) was the next test if one epoch did as well.** It did, but tonight's
   plateau and big3lr's tie both point the same way: more copies of the same sentences don't help. Low value now; better
   to spend the GPU on new sentences once there are some.
